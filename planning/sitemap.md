# Proposed RakanSales Sitemap

This sitemap is a content and navigation proposal. Only the homepage is included in the first wireframe. Inner pages will be planned after the homepage direction is approved.

## Primary Navigation

### Approved header pattern

- Navigation structure and interaction should follow respond.io's current header pattern.
- Desktop order: Product, Solutions, Resources, Pricing, Why RakanSales, Language, Contact Us, Log In.
- Product, Solutions, and Resources open grouped dropdown panels.
- Pricing and Why RakanSales are direct links.
- Contact Us is the secondary utility action; Log In is the bright primary utility action.
- RakanSales branding, Altone typography, and approved labels replace all reference-site brand assets and copy.
