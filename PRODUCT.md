# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Planned production target: a custom WordPress front end built primarily with semantic HTML, PHP templates, modern CSS, and focused JavaScript. Hosting and the final WordPress implementation approach remain open decisions. The review wireframe is a separate lightweight HTML prototype and does not constrain the production theme.

## Users

The primary audience is Malaysian SME owners evaluating how to organise and grow sales. Sales managers and enterprise decision-makers are important secondary audiences who need operational visibility, team controls, security confidence, and proof that the platform can scale.

## Product Purpose

RakanSales is a sales operating system that brings lead discovery, conversations, sales execution, automation, analytics, and extensions into one connected platform. It should help businesses capture opportunities, coordinate teams, automate repetitive work, understand performance, and close more business without stitching together fragmented tools.

## Positioning

RakanSales combines a messaging-first CRM with the broader capabilities of a sales operating system. It is built around how Malaysian businesses sell through WhatsApp, social channels, email, forms, calls, and relationship-driven workflows, while expanding into lead discovery, AI analysis, automation, and a marketplace.

## Operating Context

Teams receive leads through multiple communication and marketing channels, assign ownership, manage conversations, schedule activity, move opportunities through pipelines, follow up, measure campaign and team performance, and work from desktop or mobile. The website must support both self-directed SME evaluation and more rigorous enterprise review.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed capabilities include omnichannel conversations, shared inboxes, contacts and companies, deals and Kanban pipelines, lead assignment, AI replies and follow-ups, AI lead qualification and scoring, sales analytics, forecasting, forms, campaigns, appointments, reporting, mobile access, and integrations.

Confirmed communication and channel integrations include Website Live Chat, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, SMS, email providers such as Gmail and Outlook, custom channels through the API, Telegram, LINE, Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn. This list can expand as additional supported connections are confirmed.

Lead Finder, AI Charts, and Marketplace are committed product capabilities. Although they are being added during the website project, the new marketing experience may present them as part of the available platform rather than labelling them "coming soon." Detailed behavior and supporting content still need to be defined and reviewed.

Marketplace contains optional capabilities beyond the usual CRM requirements. The main system administrator owns and manages add-ons for the organisation. Confirmed examples include LinkedIn Enrichment, AI Voice Agent, AI Sales Advisor, AI Tokens, and AI Lead Finder. The activation, assignment, entitlement, and billing behavior still need product confirmation.

The first planning scope is the homepage and sitemap only. Inner-page wireframes are explicitly deferred. No backend database is required for the website project.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the RakanSales name, recognisable green identity, product logo, Malaysian market relevance, and the idea of a trusted sales partner. The visual redesign may substantially modernise the existing website. Reference sites are inspiration rather than templates: SleekFlow for CRM clarity, Base for confidence and restraint, Zaro for product storytelling, Clarasight for enterprise trust, and VuNet for technical depth.

## Evidence on Hand

Evidence includes the current public website and its page content, RakanSales social profiles and Linktree, three supplied product screenshots covering Conversations, Deal Pipeline, and Web Forms, mobile app listings, VeecoTech parent-company information, and existing ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and Malaysia Digital positioning.

No new customer outcomes, pricing, adoption metrics, integration promises, release dates, or testimonials have been supplied for the upcoming capabilities. Future drafts must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Present one connected sales system rather than a collection of unrelated CRM features.
- Make the value immediately understandable to an SME owner without reducing enterprise credibility.
- Demonstrate workflows through real product evidence instead of generic AI imagery or unsupported claims.
- Treat AI as useful sales infrastructure: discovery, decisions, automation, and execution.
- Keep the platform extensible so new capabilities can join without forcing another positioning rewrite.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The marketing experience must be responsive, keyboard accessible, readable at common zoom levels, compatible with reduced-motion preferences, and designed to WCAG 2.2 AA as the minimum target.
