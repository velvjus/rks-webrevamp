# RakanSales Header and Navigation Comp

Focused review artifact based on the current respond.io desktop navigation structure, translated into the RakanSales brand system.

## Pinned decisions

- Fixed header with an 8px viewport inset
- Compact dark navigation surface
- Product, Solutions and Resources use dropdown panels
- Pricing and Why RakanSales remain direct links
- Language, Contact Us and Log In form the utility group
- Altone is used throughout
- RakanSales approved logo and green replace the reference brand assets
- Brand logo remains green in both dark and bright navigation modes
- Navigation surface uses 90% opacity with frosted glass blur effect in both dark and bright modes
- Section 11 (Community & Updates) displays 4 real extracted Instagram thumbnails from @rakansales linking directly to individual posts with an animated "open in new tab" icon on hover

This is a separate comp and does not modify the production site or the read-only C4 product project.
